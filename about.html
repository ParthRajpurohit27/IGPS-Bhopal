About Our School

Indira Gandhi Public School, Prempura, Bhopal – nurturing young minds since 1993 with a vision for holistic education and excellence.

Our Story

Indira Gandhi Public School was established in 1993 by Mr. Avdhesh Rajpurohit with a vision to provide quality education to children in Prempura, Bhopal. Our school is affiliated with the MP Board and offers classes from Nursery to 8th Grade.

We focus on creating a balanced learning environment that nurtures academic excellence, physical development, and moral values. Our mission is to empower every student to become responsible citizens and lifelong learners.

  • Modern classrooms with digital learning resources
  • Experienced and caring faculty
  • Safe and secure campus
  • Focus on extracurricular and co-curricular activities
